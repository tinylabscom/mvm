// 003  Billing

How billing works.

Fixed shapes make the product legible and the bill predictable.

On Free, sandboxes won't launch again until your hours reset next billing cycle — or until you upgrade to Pro. On Pro, sandboxes keep running and overage is billed at $1.00 per sandbox-hour above your 200-hour pool. No surprise throttling, no auto-suspend mid-workload.

The meter starts when the sandbox is admitted and ready to accept workload, and stops when it terminates. Metered to the second.

No separate line items on Free or Pro — the subscription covers ephemeral disk and policy-mediated egress within published limits. Confidential computing (SEV-SNP / TDX attestation) is on the Enterprise roadmap, not shipped today; Enterprise contracts may also carve out custom egress and persistent storage terms.

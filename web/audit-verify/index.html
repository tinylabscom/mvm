mvm audit-log verifier

Verifies an mvm chain-signed audit log entirely in your browser — no upload, no server. The exact same Ed25519 chain check mvmctl audit verify runs on the host, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing you paste here leaves this tab.

Built from web/audit-verify/ over the mvm-verify crate. See ADR-070. The verifier is byte-for-byte identical to mvm_supervisor::verify_audit_chain (pinned by a cross-crate test).
